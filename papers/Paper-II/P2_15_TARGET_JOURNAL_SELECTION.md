# 10. Why not submit to Pattern Recognition first?

Pattern Recognition is tempting because of its visibility.

However, its scope guidance emphasizes genuinely original contributions to pattern-recognition theory, methodology, or application and notes that routine applications of established techniques should be directed elsewhere.

Paper 2 does have a methodological contribution.

But with only CLO-SKET as the empirical domain, reviewers could reasonably ask for evidence that the representation-selection framework generalizes to broader shape-recognition problems.

Submitting first to Pattern Recognition would therefore increase risk without necessarily increasing the scientific appropriateness of the venue.

This is not a judgment of paper quality.

It is journal-paper matching.

# 16. Potential reviewer concerns for CVIU

## Concern 1

"Why only garment sketches?"

Response:

CLO-SKET provides repeated garment identities and controlled category structure,
which permit grouped representation validation that many ordinary shape datasets
do not.

Future external validation remains explicitly acknowledged.

---

## Concern 2

"Why prespecified harmonic bands?"

Response:

The bands are part of the frozen representation framework and are not selected
post hoc according to the final outcomes.

The manuscript should describe their origin clearly.

---

## Concern 3

"Why DCT and wavelet only?"

Response:

The objective is not exhaustive basis search.

These are candidate global and localized radial representations evaluated under
one inferential framework.

The paper explicitly acknowledges that other candidate families may yield
different decisions.

---

## Concern 4

"Why is PCA-64 only 44.65%?"

Response:

No universal variance-retention sufficiency threshold is claimed.

The morphology localization analysis is explicitly conditional on PCA-64.

PCA was selected as the validated practical latent basis, not because 64 PCs were
claimed to represent total morphology.

---

## Concern 5

"Why no external dataset?"

Response:

This is the largest current limitation.

Do not hide it.

The paper's contribution is demonstrated on CLO-SKET and external replication is
a defined future test.
