# Prompt — Reconcile Deep Research and Gemini Audits

I will provide two independent audits of the same Paper II package:
1. ChatGPT Deep Research audit
2. Gemini audit

Do not vote by majority and do not average scores.

Build a reconciliation table:
`Issue | DR position | Gemini position | Frozen-source evidence | External evidence | Decision | Required action`

Rules:
- P0/P1 issues survive unless clearly refuted by source evidence.
- If one reviewer found a plausible close precedent that the other missed, investigate the precedent before dismissing it.
- Distinguish scientific validity from novelty from writing.
- Do not alter frozen numerical results.
- Do not add new experiments unless an unresolved P0/P1 issue genuinely requires one.
- Prefer the smallest defensible manuscript change.
- Mark disagreements that require human judgment.

Then return:
1. final blocker list;
2. final major-revision list;
3. minor/editorial list;
4. no-action false alarms;
5. exact manuscript patch plan;
6. whether the paper is submission-ready after those patches.
