## Required edit 1 — soften the opening universal statement

Current abstract opening:

> “Structured morphology representations are useful only if their measured organization is not silently determined by the observation frame in which the object is embedded.”

This is rhetorically strong and broader than the evidence.

### Replace with

> “Interpreting structured morphology representations requires distinguishing object-related structure from variation introduced by the observation frame.”

### Reason

The Paper-II evidence demonstrates a support dependency for the tested historical raster-relative representation; it does not establish a universal prerequisite for all structured morphology representations.

---

## Required edit 2 — make chronology explicit in the abstract

Current wording:

> “We then retained the original frozen representation-selection analysis and evaluated radial encoding separately across four prespecified angular harmonic bands…”

The phrase “we then” can imply that the support audit prospectively preceded and informed the historical representation-selection analysis.

### Replace with

> “Separately, the representation-selection analysis had already been frozen under the historical raster-relative coordinate system; it evaluated radial encoding across four prespecified angular harmonic bands using garment-identity-disjoint validation and multiplicity-controlled inference.”

### Reason

This preserves the critical anti-retroactive-selection boundary from P2_13 and P2_08–P2_11.

# Cross-checks that passed

## Title scope

PASS.

The preferred title:

## Measurement-validity claim

PASS.

The abstract correctly states that the controlled intervention altered angular spectral allocation under the historical raster-relative representation while the matched object-relative construction remained numerically invariant under the same intervention.

## Object-relative boundary

PASS.

The object-relative construction is a matched control, not a claimed new normalization method or the retroactive Paper-II replacement representation.

## Representation-selection claim

PASS.

The abstract does not claim universal descriptor superiority or intrinsic incompressibility. It states only that tested compression was supported in the low/high bands and not supported in the intermediate bands under the frozen design.

## Dimensionality statement

PASS.

The 41.98% coefficient-count reduction is presented as a dimensionality result rather than a denoising or information-removal claim.

## Latent-model boundary

PASS.

The AE/VAE result remains a non-replacement conclusion conditional on the historical hybrid; PCA is not claimed universally superior.

## Traceability boundary

PASS.

The abstract claims exact inverse localization to radial-harmonic morphology, not semantic disentanglement.

## Novelty boundary

PASS.

The title and abstract do not claim a new Fourier transform, polar coordinate system, normalization scheme, DCT, wavelet, PCA, or universal support-invariant descriptor.

## Keywords

PASS.

The keyword set now includes both the new validity layer (`representation validity`, `raster support`) and the original representation-selection layer (`evidence-controlled representation`, `spectral compression`).
