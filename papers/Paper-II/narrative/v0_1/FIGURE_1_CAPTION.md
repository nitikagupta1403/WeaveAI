**Figure 1. Following one sketch into a radial–harmonic representation.** (A) An archived garment sketch. (B) Distance and angle are measured around the intensity-weighted ink centre; the orange circle marks the shell followed in the next panels. (C) Each radial shell is unrolled into a distribution of ink over angle. An occupied shell sums to one, so this field describes angular arrangement within a shell rather than the amount of ink at each distance. (D) The angular distribution in the selected shell. (E) Its positive-frequency Fourier amplitudes: harmonic order counts angular oscillations per turn. (F) The same amplitudes across all radial shells form a radial–harmonic field. The heatmap displays magnitude; the actual representation retains complex coefficients, including phase. Radius is normalized by the complete raster-grid extent in this historical construction. The sketch is the first archived sentinel (S01, Harem/8-10.tif, row 781, support factor 1.0), chosen by archive order for illustration. It is not a population summary or a claim about garment-part semantics. Bin 36 is followed throughout. Colour scales in C and F have different quantities and are labelled separately.
