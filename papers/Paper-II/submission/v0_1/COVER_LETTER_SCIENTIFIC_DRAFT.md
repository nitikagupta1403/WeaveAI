Dear Editors,

I propose the research article “Auditing and Allocating Representation Complexity in Radial–Spectral Garment Morphology” for consideration in Computer Vision and Image Understanding.

The study examines how a structured shape representation depends on its measurement frame and how its complexity can be allocated using held-out evidence. Using 2,300 garment sketches representing 230 garment identities across 23 categories, a controlled intervention enlarges surrounding raster support while keeping garment pixels unchanged. Angular spectral allocation changes systematically under the tested historical raster-relative construction, while a matched object-relative control remains numerically invariant under the same intervention.

The original representation-selection analysis was frozen before this support audit. Its results are therefore reported conditional on the historical coordinate system: radial compression is evaluated separately across four angular harmonic bands under garment-identity-disjoint validation and multiplicity control. The selected DCT/raw/raw/wavelet representation reduces complex coefficient count by 41.98%, preserving full radial structure where tested compression lacks support. Whole-representation retrieval comparisons are explicitly descriptive, and a competitive uniform RAW42 baseline is reported. The manuscript does not claim retrieval superiority over that baseline.

Conditional on the historical hybrid, tested nonlinear encoders do not establish a multiplicity-controlled task advantage over same-dimensional PCA. Retained PCA variation is mapped exactly into radial–harmonic coordinates, providing mathematical traceability without assigning semantic garment-part meanings.

The contribution is a controlled representation-validity audit combined with evidence-controlled complexity allocation and traceable latent interpretation. Fourier transforms, normalization, DCT, wavelets, and PCA are established components rather than claimed inventions. Conclusions are bounded to the tested construction, configurations, and dataset.

I consider the manuscript relevant to the journal’s interest in shape, image representations, and image understanding. The manuscript and computational evidence are versioned, and the public research repository preserves the frozen analyses and their provenance.

I am the sole author. This research received no funding, and I declare no competing interests.
