# WeaveAI v0.2 — Concept Map

## Representation progression

Raster-relative
→ Object-relative
→ Relation-relative
→ Graph/topology-relative
→ Intrinsic metric/geodesic
→ Intrinsic spectrum
→ Diffusion coordinates
→ Grammar/program semantics

## Method families and what they contribute

### Graph Laplacian
Local difference and smoothness over connectivity.

### Graph Fourier
Variation measured relative to graph connectivity.

### Spectral graph wavelets
Structural scale + localization on the graph.

### Geodesic geometry
Shortest-path / intrinsic distance along structure.

### Diffusion geometry
Connectivity integrated over many paths and scales.

### Laplacian Eigenmaps
Coordinates derived from meaningful local adjacency.

### Mesh / manifold methods
Respect native geometric and topological domain rather than flattening to a raster.

## WeaveAI interpretation

The goal is not to choose a graph method now.

The goal is to identify which representation makes the garment problem structurally simple.
