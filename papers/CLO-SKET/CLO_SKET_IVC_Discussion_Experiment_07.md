## 5.15 Interpretation of the conventional HOG comparator

Experiment 07 is a separately frozen secondary conventional-descriptor comparison. Under its historical Experiment-06-derived fold provenance, the compact axial–radial representation produced only a negligible point increase when appended to an 8,100-dimensional HOG descriptor. The paired garment-identity bootstrap interval for the HOG+RA14-minus-HOG macro-F1 contrast crossed zero, as did the corresponding balanced-accuracy interval. The evidence therefore does not support describing RA14 as a general-purpose accuracy booster across feature families.

Experiment 07 predates the later corrective Experiment-06 identity-map repair. Its folds and historical checkpoint linkage remain frozen as Experiment-07 provenance and are not retroactively replaced by the corrected CLEAN Experiment-06 folds. Consequently, Experiment 07 should not be treated as a same-fold direct comparator for the corrected primary morphology result.

The qualitative evidence is nevertheless consistent with baseline-dependent incremental value. The corrected Experiment-06 analysis supports incremental predictive utility for complete RA14 beyond the frozen morphology representation, whereas Experiment 07 establishes no clear additional benefit beyond the tested high-dimensional HOG descriptor under its own protocol. This does not establish that RA14 information is formally redundant with HOG; the Experiment-07 interval crossing zero supports the narrower statement that an additional predictive increment was not clearly demonstrated.

The HOG result also clarifies the contribution of the study. RA14 is not claimed to outperform a conventional image descriptor in raw classification accuracy. Its value lies in providing a compact and mathematically explicit geometric measurement together with an identity-aware inferential framework. Experiment 07 remains secondary evidence and does not modify the corrected Experiment-06 claim boundary.
