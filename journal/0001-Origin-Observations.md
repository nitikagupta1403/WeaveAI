# Origin Observations

**Date:** July 2026

Today we discussed the real-world event that led to the idea of Weave AI.

## Observation 1

The inspiration for Weave AI came from a visit to a fabric store with a fashion designer.

The designer had created a hand-drawn sketch for a client in the United States.

The client asked:

> "Can you show me how this will look on me using any available tool?"

This question became the starting point of Weave AI.

---

## Observation 2

Existing tools could generate approximate visualizations.

However, they lacked the precision and personalization expected from couture fashion.

---

## Observation 3

Current virtual try-on systems focus on replacing existing garments.

A couture designer creates garments from imagination.

These are related but fundamentally different problems.

---

## Questions

- How should designer intent be represented?
- How should fabric behavior be represented?
- Can personalization become a first-class concept instead of an afterthought?

---

**Status:** Initial observations. These are hypotheses and experiences that will be validated through literature review and experiments.
