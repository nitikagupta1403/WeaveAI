# PRINCIPLES (Draft)

## Principle 1

We question to understand before we build.

## Principle 2

AI may assist our thinking, but it will never replace it.

## Principle 3

Weave AI exists to augment human creativity, never to replace it.

## Principle 4

Our goal is to bridge imagination and reality—from sketch, to garment, to the person who will wear it.

## Principle 5

Every technical decision should ultimately improve confidence for designers and customers.
