# State of the Art

## Introduction

Artificial intelligence has transformed many aspects of fashion, ranging from image generation and virtual try-on to recommendation systems and digital garment simulation. Research and commercial development have accelerated rapidly with the emergence of multimodal foundation models and generative AI. Despite these advances, most existing systems focus on improving visual realism, automation, or workflow efficiency rather than representing and reasoning about the creative intent behind fashion design.

This chapter surveys the current state of research and industrial systems relevant to Weave AI. The objective is to understand what has been achieved, identify common approaches, and establish the foundation for analysing the remaining research gaps.

# Academic Research

## Fashion Image Generation

### Overview

Describe diffusion models, GANs, and generative approaches used for fashion imagery.

### Current Capabilities

- Generate realistic fashion images
- Style transfer
- Design variations
- Image editing

### Limitations

- Focus primarily on appearance
- Limited understanding of design rationale
- No explicit representation of designer intent

## Virtual Try-On

### Overview

Summarise virtual try-on research.

### Current Capabilities

- Body-aware rendering
- Size estimation
- Pose adaptation

### Limitations

- Assumes garments already exist
- Limited support for early-stage design

## Garment Representation

### Overview

Survey how garments are currently represented.

Examples include:

- meshes
- sewing patterns
- CAD models
- point clouds
- parametric models

### Limitations

Current representations describe geometry rather than creative intent.

---

## Cloth Simulation

### Overview

Describe physical simulation.

### Current Capabilities

- Fabric deformation
- Wrinkles
- Physics simulation

### Limitations

Simulation predicts behaviour after design decisions rather than assisting those decisions.

---

## Fashion Recommendation

### Overview

Recommendation systems based on user preference.

### Current Capabilities

- Outfit recommendation
- Style similarity
- Product retrieval

### Limitations

Operate on existing products rather than new garment creation.

---

## Multimodal AI

### Overview

Large language models and vision-language models.

### Current Capabilities

- Image understanding
- Design description
- Question answering

### Limitations

General-purpose reasoning without structured fashion knowledge.

## Summary

Commercial systems have significantly improved creativity, visualisation, and production workflows. However, they primarily optimise image synthesis, simulation, or manufacturing efficiency rather than modelling designer knowledge.

# Overall Observations

Across both academia and industry, current systems demonstrate remarkable progress in generating, visualising, and simulating fashion. Nevertheless, a common pattern emerges: most approaches focus on observable outputs rather than the underlying creative reasoning that produces them.

This observation motivates the next chapter, **Research Gap**, which analyses the limitations of current approaches and identifies the scientific questions that remain unanswered.
