# The Journey

![Currie](../assets/images/Currie.jpg)

> *"Every meaningful journey begins with curiosity. Mine began with a pair of mesmerizing eyes."*

The first time I held Currie in my arms and looked into her mesmerizing eyes, I understood the true meaning of curiosity.

She was curious about everything.

Every sound.

Every new place.

Every person.

Every smell.

Every little moment was an adventure waiting to be explored.

She never settled.

There was always one more corner to discover, one more path to follow, one more reason to wag her tail with excitement.

Without ever speaking a word, Currie taught me lessons that continue to shape the way I live and learn.

She taught me unconditional love.

She taught me optimism.

She taught me to trust my journey.

She taught me that family is the greatest strength we can ever have.

Most importantly, she taught me that curiosity is not about having answers.

It is about always wanting to understand a little more than yesterday.

Looking back, I realize that the values behind Weave AI were quietly growing long before the project itself existed.

The courage to ask questions.

The excitement of discovering something new.

The willingness to keep learning.

The belief that meaningful things are created with patience, love, and perseverance.

Currie is not the reason Weave AI exists.

She is the heart behind the person building it.

Her curiosity, her playful nature, and her endless desire to explore continue to inspire every question I ask, every paper I read, every idea I pursue, and every dream I choose to believe in.

Even today, she is still teaching me.

If this repository ever becomes something meaningful, I hope it never loses the curiosity that I first saw reflected in Currie's eyes.

---

*"For Currie (24 May 2019 – 4 July 2026), who taught me that the greatest discoveries begin with curiosity, and the greatest journeys begin with love."* 🤍🐾
