# Research Questions

This document contains the fundamental questions guiding the Weave AI project.

The objective is not to answer them immediately, but to refine them through mathematics, literature review, experiments, and real-world observations.

---

## Human Representation

- How should a person's body be represented for personalized garment visualization?
- What information is necessary beyond a 2D image?
- How do morphology and posture influence garment appearance?

---

## Garment Representation

- What is the best representation of a garment before it physically exists?
- Can garments possess a canonical representation similar to SMPL for humans?
- How can sketches be transformed into structured garment representations?

---

## Fabric Representation

- How should fabric properties be represented?
- How can drape, stiffness, weight, and texture be modeled?
- Can visual appearance and physical behavior be jointly learned?

---

## Designer Intent

- How can designer intent be represented mathematically?
- Can creativity be modeled without constraining it?
- How can AI preserve artistic freedom?

---

## Customer Experience

- How can AI reduce uncertainty before manufacturing?
- How can customers better understand custom designs?
- How can AI improve confidence rather than simply generate images?

---

## Foundation Models

- What should a Fashion Foundation Model learn?
- Which variables should be modeled jointly?
- Can a unified latent representation connect people, garments, fabrics, and accessories?

---

## Long-Term Vision

How can Weave AI become an intelligent collaborator that supports designers while preserving human creativity?
