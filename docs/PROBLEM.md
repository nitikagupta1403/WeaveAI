# The Problem

Fashion is one of the few creative disciplines where the final outcome often remains uncertain until after significant investment of time, craftsmanship, and money.

For a couture designer, a sketch is only the beginning.

The final garment is influenced by many factors including fabric selection, material behavior, draping, body morphology, craftsmanship, decorative components, and countless creative decisions.

For the customer, uncertainty is even greater.

Questions naturally arise.

- Will this design suit me?
- Will this fabric behave the way I imagine?
- Will the final garment look as elegant as the sketch?
- Is it worth investing in a custom design before seeing the result?

Today's AI-powered virtual try-on systems primarily focus on replacing existing garments on a person.

However, couture design begins long before a garment exists.

The designer starts with imagination, sketches, fabrics, materials, and creative intent.

The customer wants confidence before committing to the final garment.

We believe these are fundamentally different problems.

Weave AI aims to bridge this gap by helping designers communicate their vision and helping customers make informed decisions before a single piece of fabric is cut.

Our objective is not merely to generate realistic images.

It is to reduce creative uncertainty while preserving individuality, craftsmanship, and human creativity.
