# Fashion Ontology

## Purpose

The Fashion Ontology defines the structured vocabulary used throughout the Weave AI research programme.

Its purpose is to establish a shared computational language capable of describing garments, materials, construction methods, aesthetics, body morphology, designer intent, and their relationships.

Unlike a traditional dictionary, the ontology captures both concepts and the relationships between them.

The Fashion Ontology serves as the semantic foundation for Design DNA, knowledge graphs, reasoning engines, retrieval systems, and future AI models.

---

## Objectives

- Define a common vocabulary.
- Reduce ambiguity.
- Support computational reasoning.
- Enable explainable AI.
- Facilitate interoperability.
- Provide a foundation for Design DNA.

---

## Guiding Principles

- Human understandable
- Machine readable
- Extensible
- Modular
- Evidence driven
- Independent of implementation

---

## Structure

The ontology is organised into thematic modules.

Each module defines concepts, attributes, and relationships relevant to one aspect of fashion.
